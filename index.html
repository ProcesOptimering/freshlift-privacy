Freshlift — Privacy and Support

Last updated 17 September 2026

Freshlift keeps your training on your phone. There are no accounts, and as the app stands today it makes no network requests at all.

What the app stores

Your workouts, sessions, sets, weights, notes and settings are written to a database inside the app on your device. That is the only copy we put anywhere. We cannot see it, and there is no server holding a second copy.

There is no sign-up, no email address required to use the app, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Your device backup is the one exception

If you have iCloud Backup or Android's automatic backup switched on, your phone's backup includes Freshlift's data along with everything else on the device. This is deliberate — it is how your training survives a lost or replaced phone — but it means a copy exists in the backup you already keep with Apple or Google, under their terms rather than ours. Turning that backup off, or excluding the app from it, is done in your phone's settings.

Notifications

The rest timer can notify you when a rest period ends. Those notifications are scheduled by the app on the device itself. There is no push server, and nothing is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Deleting your data

Start over in Settings erases everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the app removes it too. Because there is no copy off the device, there is nobody to write to and nothing to request — the deletion is complete when you do it.

What will change when subscriptions arrive

Freshlift is adding a paid tier. When it does, the app will check whether a purchase is valid, which means sending the store's receipt to the service that verifies it. That will be the first and only request the app makes, it carries no training data, and this page will be updated to describe it before the feature ships.

Children

Freshlift is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including them.
